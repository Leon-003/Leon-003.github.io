2026 · Research Project

An Explainability-Guided Adaptive Framework for Cross-Dataset Generalization in Bangla Sarcasm Detection

NLPTransformersExplainable AICross-Dataset
Problem

Strong in-domain performance does not necessarily translate to reliable performance across independently constructed Bangla sarcasm datasets.

Approach

Adaptive cross-dataset exposure combined with explainability-guided analysis of feature transferability, explanation agreement, and stability.

Contribution

A framework for evaluating and improving cross-dataset generalization while making model behavior more interpretable.
